RONDELLE — file converter with a wheel
=======================================

Runs on macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3…) and Intel Macs.

INSTALL
1. Drag «Rondelle» onto «Applications» (next to it in this window).
2. Open Rondelle from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the developer: Rondelle isn't
   distributed through the App Store and isn't notarized by Apple. Click «Done» (not «Move to Trash»).
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll to «Security»: next to «Rondelle was blocked…» click
   «Open Anyway» and confirm with your password. This is only needed once.
4. Rondelle lives in the menu bar (ring icon). Drag a file in the Finder while holding ⇧ Shift: the wheel of
   formats appears around the pointer.

PRIVACY
Conversions have no Internet access (the macOS sandbox blocks it). Rondelle collects no data automatically and
writes only to the folders you allow. The form in Settings → Help and contact sends, through another separate
process, only what you write and only when you press Send. Once a day a small separate process, with no access to
your files, asks GitHub for the latest version (turn it off in Settings → Privacy). Updates don't install
themselves: download the new version and replace the app in Applications. Translation uses Apple's service,
which according to Apple works on the device.

UNINSTALL
Quit Rondelle (menu bar icon → Quit) and move the app to the Trash.
